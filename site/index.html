Zebra GitHub

Your Helio Strap,
on your Mac.

A free, open-source app for the Amazfit Helio Strap. Recovery, sleep and body age. Live heart rate straight from the strap. An AI coach that knows your numbers.

Download for Mac Star on GitHub

Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later. 28 MB. Windows is not available yet.

Everything the strap knows.

Zebra downloads your Zepp history into a database on your Mac, talks to the strap over Bluetooth, and explains it in plain English.

Today

Sleep, readiness, body age and steps, plus six vitals with a note on each, like "3 beats higher than usual".

Insights

This week against last week, a daily read, a weekly plan, and an illness early warning.

Live, always on

Zebra stays connected to the strap and saves about one heart rate reading every second.

Body age

From sleep, consistency, steps, resting heart rate, HRV and PAI. Smoothed, so it does not jump.

Coach

An iMessage-style chat about your data. It runs on the AI you choose.

Strap settings

Change the heart rate interval and alerts on the strap, from your Mac.

Claude Code on your wrist

One buzz when a task finishes. Two buzzes when Claude needs you.

Yours

Local SQLite. No account with us, no server of ours. MIT licensed.

Any AI. Or none.

Use an API key from Sarvam, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google Gemini or Groq. Or run a model free on your Mac with Ollama or LM Studio. Or point it at any OpenAI-compatible server. Only your numbers are sent, and only when you ask.

Install in a minute.

  1. Download Zebra.dmg, open it, and drag Zebra into Applications.
  2. The app is not notarized yet. Run this once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Zebra.app. Or open it once, then click Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy & Security.
  3. Open Zebra and sign in with your Zepp account in Settings. Your history syncs.
  4. Allow Bluetooth. The Live page shows your heart rate.

The extra strap features need a small Python client. The README explains it.
